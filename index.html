Overview

Student Outcomes

Output-centered learning. The tools support the work while student thinking, decisions, and finished outcomes stay at the center.

Each unit builds toward something students can share, test, explain, and improve—a designed product, coded experience, robotic system, digital response, or reflective portfolio.

01 / Learning Area

Design Tech

From design brief to tested product. Students respond to a need, develop an idea, make a solution, and improve it through evidence.

Learning focus

Design with purpose.

Middle school learners create for an audience, communicate their choices, and treat each prototype as a chance to learn.

Supporting resources

CAD: Adobe, Onshape, Tinkercad

Making: laser cutter, 3D printer, sewing machine, Cricut

Materials: cardboard, textile, vinyl, wood

Student outputs

What students produce

  • Design briefs, sketches, research, and CAD models
  • Functional products and prototypes for a defined audience
  • Testing evidence, user feedback, and documented changes
  • Final evaluations that explain learning and next steps
Design in action

Making ideas tangible.

Follow the learning journey from client research and CAD to prototyping, feedback, testing, and sharing.

02 / Learning Area

Computer Science / Coding

Build interactive work, not just write code. Students create, debug, explain, and improve programs for people to use.

Learning focus

Code for an audience.

Projects move from visual logic to text-based programming and web creation while keeping problem-solving and user experience visible.

Student outputs

What students produce

  • Playable stories, games, and interactive experiences
  • Python programs that solve defined problems
  • HTML pages that communicate ideas clearly
  • Debugging notes, peer testing, and improved versions

03 / Learning Area

Robotics and Circuits

Engineer, test, and compete together. Classroom projects, team challenges, clubs, and competitions make collaboration and iteration essential.

Learning contexts

Integrated. Team-based. Authentic.

Students connect mechanisms, sensors, circuitry, and code to create systems that must work—not just look complete.

Integrated units Team projects Clubs Competitions
Student outputs

What students produce

  • Robots and circuits that respond to a clear challenge
  • Controllers, sensor systems, and programmed behaviors
  • Team plans, test data, match strategy, and engineering evidence
  • Competition-ready solutions strengthened through repeated trials

Learning Framework

Learning in MS

Standards, access, feedback, and reflection. A clear framework keeps ambitious projects purposeful, inclusive, and assessable.

01 / STANDARDS

Learning Standards-Based Units

CSTA and ISTE standards make learning goals and progression visible.

02 / DESIGN & ACCESS

Structured design learning with multiple entry points and support for diverse learners.

Students document evidence, explain changes, and identify their next steps.
